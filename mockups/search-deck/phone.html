Search Deck on iPhone

Mock-up v2, 2026-11-21 (after Gayble’s review: drawn keyboard, two-line snippets centred on the match, 44 pt tap areas, Clear as a text button, one type label per row), companion to the desktop Search Deck mock-up. The Threads screen, thread view, title bar, tab bar and sheets are the real phone app's markup and styles. Search pieces are new. All threads and results are sample data.

  1. Tap Search Deck under the Threads title.
  2. Type installer. Results arrive grouped by type, three per group, with Show all for more.
  3. Under Messages, tap “Try the installer on a clean Mac before signing off.” Release handoff opens scrolled to that reply, which flashes mint. Back returns to your results.
  4. Tap More… and turn on Include Trash, then tap Old installer checklist to see the restore sheet.

Also shown: filter chips, recent searches and recently opened when the field is empty, the offline-machine line with Retry, and the empty state (try zzz).

Left out on purpose: the desktop's “This project” scope (the Threads tab has no current project), and a search button on the other tabs (one entry point first; we can add a magnifier to Agents, Code, Tasks and Library if you want it everywhere).
