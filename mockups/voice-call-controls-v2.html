Voice call controls v2: one button, mic or Send

New in v2: there’s no separate Send button. The round button is the mic while the box is empty and turns into Send as soon as you type, paste or attach something. Clear the box and it goes back to the mic. Enter still sends, and each bot keeps its own stop button, so Send never has to be Stop.

The three call looks are unchanged from v1: each keeps only who you’re talking to, what they’re doing, Mute and End on screen.

  • Try it: type in any chat box below and watch the mic turn into Send. Delete the text and it turns back.
  • Use the buttons below to switch every option to the same moment in a call.
  • Built from Deck’s real thread view in your Clay skin. The bot reply and names are sample text.

Call bar on top of the chat box

A slim bar sits right above where you type, for as long as the call runs. You see who’s on, what they’re doing and the timer at a glance, and you can still type.

Good: Clearest at a glance. Settings are one tap away.Cost: The chat box grows by one slim line during a call.
B

The mic turns into a call pill

Nothing new appears. The mic beside Send becomes a small pill with Mute, a live sound wave plus timer, and End. What the bot is doing shows inside the chat box, where you’d type.

Good: Smallest. Feels like ChatGPT and Claude’s voice buttons.Cost: The status line hides once you start typing.
C

A call capsule in the thread’s top bar

A small capsule sits in the middle of the thread’s top bar, a bit like the iPhone’s Dynamic Island. The chat box doesn’t change. Tap the capsule to open settings.

Good: Chat box stays exactly as it is today.Cost: Far from the mic and chat box, and tight in narrow panes.

Same in all three

  • Settings are one small panel: who you’re talking to, that bot’s voice with a preview, the pause slider and nicknames.
  • Tapping the mic in a thread with several bots asks “Call who?” first (see “Tap mic: call who?”).
  • Left as is on purpose: the one-time voice download box. It only shows once, so it isn’t part of this redesign.
