Phone voice calls: the Mac's three looks

Built from the real iPhone thread screen (captured from the app, on a throwaway test server). Same three looks as the Mac, reshaped for a phone. The thread here is on MacBook Pro, so this is also what a phone-to-Mac call would look like.

  • The old tall call sheet is gone. During a call you only see who you're talking to, what they're doing, a timer, Mute and End.
  • Voice, pause, nicknames and the look all sit in one Call settings sheet that slides up, like every other phone sheet.
  • Settings are shared with the Mac, so voices and the pause carry over.

Phone-only differences from the Mac

  • Pill: the TL;DR switch steps aside during a call so the pill fits next to Send. It comes back when the call ends.
  • Top bar: the capsule takes the place of the "project · machine" line under the title, and has its own small End button, since the chat box is far from your thumb otherwise.
  • Every look: tapping the call (strip, wave or capsule) opens Call settings. Mute and End are also at the top of that sheet.
  • Calls still only run with the app open on screen. Locking the phone ends the call, and the bot keeps working.
