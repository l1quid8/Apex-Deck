Machine Rail

Jigga's take. Same data and menus the list has today, arranged by where the work runs. Tap a project to fold it, hold a row for its menu, try + for New thread.

  • Recent goes first and sorts by what needs you: waiting, then working, then newest. Four rows, same as today.
  • Machines replace the Projects header. The machine name is said once, not on every project, and its projects hang off a rail coloured by its link: mint online, amber asleep, dashed grey offline.
  • An offline machine says so once, in its own group, with Retry. Mac threads stay normal, just like the app's pause rules.
  • Closed projects show a count, the latest age, and a dot if a bot is working or waiting inside. Open projects get ⋯ and a New thread line at the end.
  • Hold any thread or project for its menu (real items only).
